# VacationPro Deal Engine: 6-Month Revenue Forecast

> Models incremental revenue from the VacationPro deal engine, May to November 2026. Built on labeled assumptions, not actuals. Adjust the assumption tables and the monthly numbers flow from them. Companion to `vacationpro-deal-engine-strategy.md` and `vacationpro-rollout-timeline.md`.

**Important:** This forecasts revenue *driven by the deal engine*. Any existing VacationPro baseline revenue (current Tristar leads, current FB Creator income, current newsletter and sponsorship income) is additive on top and should be filled in once known. All inputs below are assumptions, marked so you can replace them.

## 8. Summary

| Scenario | 6-month total | Month 6 run rate (annualized) |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative | ~$22,235 | ~$68,000/yr |
| Base | ~$72,330 | ~$214,000/yr |
| Aggressive | ~$241,145 | ~$704,000/yr |

The single biggest lever is **keyword comments per deal post** (12 vs 25 vs 50). It moves Tristar revenue, which is the largest stream in every scenario. The second lever is **lead-to-webinar conversion**, since that is the gate on the $250 payout. Focus early optimization there: hook quality, keyword clarity, and DM-to-landing-page friction.

## 9. How to use this

1. Replace the assumption tables (Sections 1-3) with real numbers as VacationPro's actuals come in.
2. The monthly tables (Sections 5-7) are derived from those assumptions plus the ramp schedule. Recompute them when assumptions change.
3. Add existing baseline VacationPro revenue on top: this forecast is incremental from the deal engine only.
4. Re-baseline after the first weekly analytics loop in Phase 2: that is the first real data point for comments-per-post and funnel conversion.
