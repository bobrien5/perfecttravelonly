# VacationPro — Business & Execution Plan

*Canonical business plan. Latest revision 2026-05-15. Owners: Brendan O'Brien, Jordan. Companion execution roadmap: [`vacationpro-summer-10k-execution.md`](vacationpro-summer-10k-execution.md).*

## Brand positioning

What VacationPro actually is:

- **Not** a traditional travel agency.
- **Not** an influencer page.
- **Not** just a deals account.

VacationPro should become a **modern travel media + concierge brand** that helps people discover, plan, and book aspirational vacations.

**Long term:**
- Media company
- Concierge travel brand
- Digital products business
- Creator education ecosystem

**Phase 1 (now):** Build a profitable audience-first travel brand to **$10k/month**.

---

## Phase 1 goal (0–12 months)

**Revenue target:** $10k/month, via:
- Digital products
- Concierge travel commissions
- Sponsorships
- Affiliate income

## Content pillars

### Pillar 1 — Dream / Aspiration

Examples:
- Best Caribbean islands
- Cheapest tropical destinations
- Best adults-only resorts
- Maldives alternatives
- Best beaches rankings
- Honeymoon destinations

**Grows fastest.**

### Pillar 2 — Travel deals

Examples:
- "Jamaica all-inclusive for $899"
- "Flights to Aruba under $300"
- "55% off resorts"

**High engagement + booking intent.**

### Pillar 3 — Travel planning tips

Examples:
- Best times to book
- Avoiding resort scams
- Airport hacks
- Carry-on tips
- Passport / travel rules

**Builds authority.**

### Pillar 4 — Luxury travel lifestyle

Examples:
- Beach POVs
- Resort walkthroughs
- "Day in the life"
- Aesthetic travel edits

**Attracts sponsors later.**

### Pillar 5 — Guides & recommendations

Examples:
- "Best resorts in Cancun"
- "Top Caribbean honeymoon resorts"
- "Where to stay in Aruba"

**Converts into:** guides, affiliate revenue, concierge leads.

## Revenue streams

### 1. Digital products *(fastest scalable revenue)*

This is the **first monetization layer**.

**Products to launch first (low-ticket, $5–6):**
- Caribbean Resort Guide
- Best All-Inclusive Resorts Guide
- Budget vs Luxury destination guides
- `[Destination]` Guides for 2026

**Sell via:** Beacons

### 2. Concierge travel services

**Positioning:**
- ❌ NOT "cheap vacations."
- ✅ YES curated stress-free travel planning.

**Focus on:**
- All-inclusives
- Honeymoons
- Group trips
- Luxury tropical travel
- Cruises

**Higher-ticket trips = bigger commissions.**

**Offer structure:**
- Free inquiry call → itinerary planning → resort recommendations → booking support → VIP planning
- Can charge planning fees later, earn commissions, upsell experiences

### 3. Affiliate income

**Start immediately.**

Best categories:
- Luggage
- Amazon travel essentials
- Travel credit cards
- Resorts / hotels
- Viator / GetYourGuide
- Travel insurance
- Cruise lines

### 4. Sponsorships

*(Comes after audience traction.)*

Target:
- Luggage brands
- Resorts
- Cruise companies
- Travel accessories
- Travel apps

## Audience growth strategy

### Daily content target

| Platform | Cadence |
|---|---|
| TikTok | 2–4 / day |
| Instagram | 2 Reels / day + stories consistently |
| Facebook | Repurpose everything (travel performs extremely well there) |

### Best content formats

| Format | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Ranking videos** ("Top 10 Caribbean islands") | Already working |
| **Comparison graphics** ("Budget vs Luxury", "Cheapest vs Most Expensive") | High save/share |
| **Deal graphics** | High save/share potential |
| **POV / aesthetic travel clips** | Critical for brand feel |
| **Carousel posts** | Instagram loves these now |

## Lead generation system

**Goal:** get people off social platforms.

```
Viral content
     │
     ▼
Free lead magnet
  e.g. "Top 25 Caribbean Resorts"
       "Free Honeymoon Planning Guide"
       "Cheapest Tropical Destinations"
     │
     ▼
Email list  (Beehiiv)
     │
     ▼
Sell: guides · concierge services · affiliates
```
